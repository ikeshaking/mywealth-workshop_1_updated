Step 1 of 8 — Clients & Import

Preview

📘 SOP — Debt Transfer Instructions Generator

Standard operating procedure for producing a client Debt Transfer Instructions guide (debt recycling). Work through the wizard steps left to right — the preview on the right always shows exactly what will export. Allow ~10 minutes with settlement details on hand; confirm every imported or calculated figure before the guide leaves the office.

Before you start — what you need

  • Loan settlement details — the lender's settlement PDF or the broker's "Settlement details" email (balances, repayment, account numbers, rates).
  • Budget review — the client's confirmed weekly surplus.
  • Plan costs — weekly insurance premiums, advice fees, gearing/investment costs and (if any) investment property costs, plus the client's marginal tax rate.
  • Platform details — which investment platform (e.g. Hub24) and the agreed initial and monthly investment amounts.
  • If this updates an earlier guide, the client's previous .json backup file — load it via 📂 Load JSON in the top bar instead of re-keying.

0 · Import from Loan Settlementrecommended

On Step 1 — Clients & Import, drop the settlement document onto the Smart import zone (or click Choose file…). Two formats are recognised automatically:

  • Split schedules ("Split 1: Amount… Loan account number… Linked offset account…") — each split is classified automatically: interest-only splits go to the Share Investment Loan, P&I splits to the Home Loan, and splits secured against a different address become Investment Property Loans.
  • Settlement-summary emails ("Settlement details" with Loan Amount / Linked Offset / Repayment Amount lines) — fills the client names, Home Loan balance, monthly P&I repayment (weekly/fortnightly amounts are converted), and the last 4 digits of the loan and offset accounts. These emails don't state the interest rate — enter the rate manually.

The status line under the zone lists exactly what was filled (including the due date, repayment account and lender where found). Always confirm the imported figures against the lender — the import is a head start, not a source of truth. If the file can't be read, expand …or paste text, paste the settlement text and click Parse. To restart completely, use 🗑 Clear form & start fresh.

1 · Clients & Import

Enter the client name(s) exactly as they should appear in the guide (first names are fine — they print in the cover line "Prepared for …" and the Word/JSON filenames). Leave Client 2 blank for sole clients. Enter the adviser name — it appears in the guide's introduction and sign-off. Pick the office — it sets the corporate authorised representative line in the document footer (roster on the ⚙ Admin page). Tick refresh only when this is an update to an existing debt recycling structure: it switches the intro wording from "new strategy" to "refreshed strategy".

2 · Investments

Enter the initial investment transfer (one-off lump sum, if any) and the monthly ongoing investment — the weekly equivalent is derived automatically (monthly × 12 ÷ 52) and used in the transfer instructions. Pick the investment platform; the name flows through to the instructions, flowchart and account structure. These amounts must match what was agreed in the advice document — the guide is an implementation instruction, not a place to revise the strategy.

3 · Home Loans

Three groups, all driving the account structure and transfer steps:

  • Home Loan — balance, monthly P&I payment, rate and the last 4 digits of the account (use "TBC" if not yet known).
  • Home Loan Offset — current balance and last 4 digits. Wages land here; extra repayments are made from here.
  • Share Investment Loan — the facility that funds the investing: Limit (approved facility), Balance (already drawn); Available is calculated automatically. Add its offset account and balance, and any additional share investment loans (e.g. from a refresh). The Cash Management Account (MCMA) last-4 completes the structure.

4 · Property Loans

Add one entry per investment property loan — description (the property), balance, monthly interest payment, rate, account and offset details. Each appears in the account structure, the interest-redirection steps and the flowchart. Whether their running costs sit inside or outside the plan is set on the Costs step.

5 · Costs

This step sets the weekly cashflow engine of the guide:

  • Savings capacity — the weekly surplus from the budget review. The monthly figure is derived automatically.
  • Tax rates — marginal rate (excl. Medicare) plus the Medicare levy (default 2%); the effective rate drives the after-tax calculations and the annual tax refund estimate.
  • Weekly costs breakdown — enter weekly before-tax amounts on the left (insurance, advice fees, gearing, property costs). After-tax amounts for advice, gearing and property are calculated automatically; insurance after-tax must be entered manually (premiums are usually IP + trauma, and the trauma component isn't deductible, so it can't be derived). No period conversion is applied — these figures pull straight through to the weekly MCMA transfer.
  • Exclude Investment Property Costs — tick when the client manages property costs outside the plan; they then drop out of the MCMA transfer and the flowchart shows the property as "managed separately".
  • Home loan additional repayments — calculated automatically as surplus less plan costs; this is the "extra on top of minimum" figure in the guide and flowchart.

6 · Instructions

Each toggle produces a numbered step in the client guide — untick anything not relevant to this client:

  • MCMA Buffer — buffer amount and which account funds it.
  • Upfront Advice Fee — amount and funding account.
  • Home Loan Status & Extra Repayments — always included; optionally add the surplus cashflow subnote.
  • Weekly Cost Transfers — the weekly transfer into the MCMA; optionally include insurance in the MCMA costs and add an insurance note.
  • Monthly Investment Transfer — timing and cutoff day for the platform investment.
  • Investment Loan Interest Redirection — set the status (possible / not possible with this lender); this also changes the flowchart's cost boxes.
  • Rental Income → MCMA and Investment Property Expenses — property cashflow steps (only when properties exist).
  • Annual Tax Refund — what to do with the refund each year.
  • Custom Instructions — add client-specific steps with your own title and wording.

7 · Flowchart

The linked balances at the top are read-only checks — they mirror what you entered earlier. Toggle the Strategy & Benefits row items (Super per client, Insurance, Tax Savings) and add custom strategy boxes if needed. The flowchart rebuilds automatically from the loan, cost and instruction settings — if something looks wrong here, fix it on the earlier step rather than working around it.

8 · Summary & Export

Review the cashflow summary (weekly/monthly/annual before and after, tax refunds) against the advice document, then:

  • 📄 Generate Guide — builds the full preview on the right. Read it top to bottom.
  • 📥 Download Word — exports the branded document, auto-named Debt_Transfer_<Clients>_<date>.doc.
  • 💾 Save Backup (.json) — downloads the working file, named identically to the Word document.
  • ✉️ Copy for Email — copies the formatted guide for pasting straight into Outlook/Gmail, or print to PDF from the preview.

QA checklist before sending: client names spelt correctly · balances, rates and account last-4s match the lender · weekly commitment matches the budget review · every included instruction step reads correctly for this client · flowchart shows the right accounts and costs · adviser name and sign-off correct.

💾 Important — always download a JSON copy of your work. Before you close this page, click 💾 Save JSON (top bar) or Save Backup (.json) on the Summary step. It downloads a small .json working file containing everything you have entered. The JSON file is named exactly the same as your exported Word document — it just ends in .json — save both to the client's folder together. To pick up where you left off (or run next year's refresh), open this page and use 📂 Load JSON. The browser auto-draft is a convenience only — it lives on this device and can be lost; the JSON file is your reliable copy.

Troubleshooting

  • "No loan details found" on import — the PDF is likely a scan or an unusual layout; expand …or paste text and paste the settlement text instead.
  • "Could not load the PDF reader" — the PDF library loads from the internet on first use; check the connection or use the paste-text option.
  • Import filled the wrong fields — press Ctrl/⌘+Z to undo, or 🗑 Clear form & start fresh and enter manually.
  • Preview is empty / Word file is blank — click 📄 Generate Guide first, then export.
  • Figures look stale — every field recalculates on change; if something looks cached, click Generate Guide again.

Shortcuts & tips

Ctrl/⌘+Z undo · Ctrl+Y / ⌘⇧Z redo · 🌙 toggles the night theme (interface only — the exported document is unaffected). Inputs auto-save to this browser as you type. 🔄 Reset All (Summary step) clears everything for a new client — save the JSON first.

Time Out of Market Calculator

Standardised opportunity-cost model for contributions left uninvested (or invested off-schedule) — based on the E & A Sibly (cash basis) and J & A Wilson (units basis) workbooks.

Getting the data: a local HTML page cannot fetch Vanguard's price or distribution history directly — browsers block cross-site requests (CORS) and Vanguard has no public API for this. Instead, download the fund's price history and distribution history exports from the fund's page on vanguard.com.au (Prices & distributions section), or from the platform (CFS Edge transaction/valuation reports), then upload the CSV below or simply copy the rows in Excel and paste them — the calculator detects the columns and pre-populates automatically.

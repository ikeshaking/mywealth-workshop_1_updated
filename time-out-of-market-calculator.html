Time Out of Market Calculator

Standardised opportunity-cost model for money that was out of the market — for any client. Built from the E & A Sibly (cash basis) and J & A Wilson (units basis) Vanguard workbooks, extended with lump-sum, fund-switch and lump-sum-vs-phasing scenarios.

Getting the data: download the fund's price history and distribution history exports from vanguard.com.au (Prices & distributions), or the platform's transaction/valuation reports (CFS Edge etc.), then upload the file or paste the rows straight from Excel below — the calculator detects the columns and pre-populates. (A local page can't fetch Vanguard directly: browsers block cross-site requests and there's no public price API, so upload/paste is the reliable route.)

The switch

Sells at the Sell price, sits in cash over the gap, buys back at the Buy price. The loss captures the round-trip spread plus market drift, less interest earned, plus any distribution missed while out.
